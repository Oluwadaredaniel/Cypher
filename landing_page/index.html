Privacy Promise

What CYPHER collects about you.

Short answer: nothing. Long answer: also nothing. Here's exactly how it works.

CYPHER is a local-network application. Every byte of data — files, clipboard content, notifications, commands — travels directly between your phone and your PC over your own WiFi. There is no CYPHER server in the middle. There is no account. There is no analytics beacon.

  • 🔒
    No external servers All traffic stays on your local network. CYPHER has no backend infrastructure to send data to.
  • 👤
    No accounts required No email, no sign-up, no profile. You are not a user in any database anywhere.
  • 📊
    No analytics or tracking No usage stats, no crash reports phoning home, no telemetry. CYPHER does not know you exist.
  • 🗑️
    Uninstall = total removal Nothing persists on external servers because nothing was ever sent there. Uninstall and it's like CYPHER never existed.

Getting Started

No accounts. No configuration. No headaches.

01

Grab the .exe for your Windows PC and the .apk for your Android phone from the GitHub releases page.

FAQ

Questions people
actually ask.

Honest answers. No corporate speak.

Does CYPHER work without internet?
Yes, completely. CYPHER communicates directly between your phone and PC over your local WiFi or phone hotspot. No internet connection is ever required for any feature.
How fast are file transfers actually?
On a typical phone hotspot over 5GHz WiFi, you can expect 20–50 MB/s — fast enough to transfer a 1GB file in about 30 seconds. Speed depends on your hotspot hardware.
Is my data safe? Who can see my files?
Only devices you explicitly pair. CYPHER uses token-based authentication — random devices on the same network cannot access your files. No external party can ever see your data because it never leaves your network.
Does the PC have to stay on?
Yes — your PC needs to be powered on and running the CYPHER app for the connection to work. You can use CYPHER to put your PC to sleep remotely, but you can't wake it up (hardware limitation).
What file types are supported?
All of them. CYPHER transfers raw file bytes — documents, videos, images, executables, archives, anything. There are no restrictions on file type or size.
Do I need a Google account or subscription?
No accounts, no subscriptions, no sign-ups. CYPHER is completely free and requires nothing except the apps themselves. There is no premium tier.
What happens if the connection drops?
CYPHER automatically detects disconnection and shows a reconnection screen. Active transfers can be retried. The app remembers your PC address so reconnecting is one tap.
Can multiple phones connect at once?
Currently one primary device at a time. Guest access allows additional temporary connections but with limited folder access. Multi-device support is planned for a future release.
